<En>
**In ten seconds:** Genesis Town is a pixel-art market town that runs in the browser.
Players gather fish, ore, wood and wheat, craft food and tools, and trade with four AI
merchants who set their own prices. Items and coins live on a NEAR contract, but players
sign in with Google or email and never see a seed phrase, a wallet popup or a gas prompt.
Built by a team of three for the NEAR Hackathon (November 1 to 14, 2026), Games With Real
Economies track.

<Room title="Problem">


Most on-chain games either put a token on top of a static game, or make players fight
wallets and gas before they can play. Either way the chain is in the way, and the
economy is decoration.


</Room>

<Room title="Solution">


Make the economy the game, and keep the chain out of sight:

- Each merchant prices an item from base price, its own stock and recent demand. A price
  moves at most 10% per game hour and stays between a quarter and four times the base.
  Every change comes with a one-sentence reason in a tooltip.
- Merchants pay daily rent. One that can't pay closes, and players can invest coins to
  reopen it in exchange for shares that pay part of the shop's daily profit.
- Seasons of seven game days change what the land yields and what the town buys.

```text
gather / craft / merchant trade   → instant, off-chain
once per game day (24 real min)   → one settlement transaction on NEAR
player-to-player trades, shares   → on-chain immediately
gas                               → paid by a relayer, never by the player
```


</Room>

<Room title="Decisions and tradeoffs">


- **Settle once a day, not every action.** Gathering and crafting stay instant. The chain
  receives each day's net changes in one transaction, with a hash of the published
  ledger so anyone can check it.
- **A bounded money supply.** The only new coins come from townsfolk buying finished
  goods, capped per day and throttled as supply nears a cap. Rent, crafting fees, a 5%
  market fee, eaten food and tool wear take coins back out.
- **One pricing rule, two users.** The same pricing code runs live play and a
  fast-forward simulator, so economy changes are tested over many simulated days
  before players feel them.


</Room>

<Room title="Status">


In development. The game contract, relayer and settlement keys are deployed on NEAR
testnet, and the repository holds the design docs: game loop, economy, architecture and
threat model.

</Room>

</En>

<Id>
**Dalam sepuluh detik:** Genesis Town adalah kota pasar pixel-art yang berjalan di
browser. Pemain mengumpulkan ikan, bijih, kayu, dan gandum, membuat makanan dan alat,
lalu berdagang dengan empat pedagang AI yang menentukan harga sendiri. Item dan koin ada
di kontrak NEAR, tapi pemain cukup masuk dengan Google atau email tanpa pernah melihat
seed phrase, popup dompet, atau biaya gas. Dibangun oleh tim bertiga untuk NEAR Hackathon
(1 sampai 14 November 2026), track Games With Real Economies.

<Room title="Masalah">


Kebanyakan game on-chain hanya menempelkan token di atas game yang statis, atau memaksa
pemain berurusan dengan dompet dan gas sebelum bisa bermain. Akhirnya blockchain
menghalangi, dan ekonominya cuma hiasan.


</Room>

<Room title="Solusi">


Jadikan ekonomi sebagai permainannya, dan sembunyikan blockchain-nya:

- Tiap pedagang menghitung harga dari harga dasar, stoknya sendiri, dan permintaan
  terbaru. Harga bergerak paling banyak 10% per jam game dan tetap di antara seperempat
  sampai empat kali harga dasar. Setiap perubahan disertai satu kalimat alasan.
- Pedagang membayar sewa harian. Yang tidak sanggup membayar akan tutup, dan pemain bisa
  menanam koin untuk membukanya lagi dengan imbalan saham dari keuntungan harian toko.
- Musim selama tujuh hari game mengubah hasil bumi dan barang yang dibeli kota.

```text
kumpul / buat / dagang dengan pedagang  → instan, off-chain
sekali per hari game (24 menit nyata)    → satu transaksi settlement di NEAR
dagang antarpemain, saham                → langsung on-chain
gas                                      → dibayar relayer, bukan pemain
```


</Room>

<Room title="Keputusan & Kompromi">


- **Settlement sekali sehari, bukan tiap aksi.** Mengumpulkan dan membuat barang tetap
  instan. Blockchain menerima perubahan bersih tiap hari dalam satu transaksi, beserta
  hash ledger yang dipublikasikan agar siapa pun bisa memeriksanya.
- **Jumlah uang yang terbatas.** Koin baru hanya muncul dari warga yang membeli barang
  jadi, dibatasi per hari dan diperlambat saat pasokan mendekati batas. Sewa, biaya
  membuat barang, biaya pasar 5%, makanan yang dimakan, dan alat yang aus menarik koin
  kembali.
- **Satu aturan harga, dua pemakai.** Kode harga yang sama dipakai di permainan langsung
  dan di simulator percepatan, jadi perubahan ekonomi diuji selama banyak hari simulasi
  sebelum pemain merasakannya.


</Room>

<Room title="Status">


Masih dalam pengembangan. Kontrak game, relayer, dan kunci settlement sudah terpasang di
NEAR testnet, dan repositori berisi dokumen desain: alur permainan, ekonomi, arsitektur,
dan model ancaman.

</Room>
